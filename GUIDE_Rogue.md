# NetHack Ascension Roadmap: Rogue

The Rogue is a skilled, sneaky character who rewards patient, tactical play.
Not as tough as the Fighter, not as magically powerful as the Wizard, but with
unique advantages: backstab multipliers that can one-shot strong monsters,
built-in stealth, and an excellent quest artifact. This is a moderately difficult
role — easier than Wizard early, harder than Fighter overall.

---

## Character Overview

**Alignment:** Chaotic  
**Starting stats:** Good Dex, decent Int, low Str and Con  
**Starting gear:** Short sword (+0), two daggers, leather armor, cloak of displacement (often),
some gold pieces  
**Pet:** None  
**Quest artifact:** Master Key of Thievery — opens any lock; when invoked, teleports you
(with control if you have ring of teleport control); also provides stealth and teleportitis  

**Key intrinsic advantages:**
- Stealth from the start (or very early) — monsters don't wake up as easily
- Can disarm traps reliably at higher levels
- Dagger throwing: multiple daggers thrown in a volley deal massive damage (throwing 8 daggers
  at once can instakill many mid-game monsters)
- Backstab: attacking a monster that hasn't seen you multiplies damage significantly

**Role strengths:**
- Very safe early game due to stealth and leather armor AC
- Cloak of displacement causes monsters to miss more often (displacement = appear elsewhere)
- Dagger multi-throw is one of the best ranged attacks in the game
- Master Key of Thievery makes locked doors and chests trivial
- Can pick locks without a key (using a dagger or lockpick)

---

## The Backstab Mechanic

Backstab is the Rogue's defining ability. When you attack a monster that:
- Has not yet detected you (sleeping, unaware, or you're behind it), OR
- Has been made unaware (by sleeping it, confusing it, etc.)

...the attack deals significantly multiplied damage (up to 9x depending on level).

**Practical use:**
- Throw a dart or arrow into a sleeping monster to wake it, then step adjacent while it's
  re-orienting (it hasn't seen you yet): backstab
- Use a wand of sleep, potion of sleeping, or spellbook of sleep on a monster: backstab
- Sneak up from behind in a corridor while stealthy: backstab
- Any monster that doesn't actively see you can be backstabbed

A high-level Rogue backstabbing with a +5 short sword can instakill most non-boss monsters.

---

## The Dagger Multi-Throw

Rogues have a natural aptitude for daggers. When you throw a stack of daggers:
- All daggers in the stack are thrown simultaneously
- Each one can hit for short sword-level damage
- Collecting 8+ daggers and throwing the full stack deals massive burst damage

**Practical setup:**
- Keep a stack of 8–15 daggers in inventory (they're light)
- Enchant them to +5 with scrolls of enchant weapon (affects all in the stack)
- A stack of 8 blessed +5 daggers can kill most Gehennom demons in one throw

## Stage 1: Early Dungeon (Dungeon Levels 1–6)

**Goal:** Establish stealth-first play style, collect daggers, improve AC.

### What to do
- Fight from stealth whenever possible — step into corridors where monsters haven't seen you
- Throw daggers at monsters in doorways before they reach you (ranged backstab if they're
  still unaware)
- Collect every dagger you see — this is your primary long-term weapon investment
- Identify items by use: Rogues can sometimes learn what a potion/scroll does from using it
  (quaff unidentified potions when HP is full; read unidentified scrolls when not carrying
  anything valuable in case it's a scroll of fire)
- AC target early: get below 5 with leather armor + cloak + anything else you find

### Cloak of displacement
Your starting cloak of displacement is very valuable:
- Makes monsters appear displaced (they attack where you aren't)
- Even strong monsters miss frequently while this is on
- Keep it on; don't swap for a cloak of magic resistance until you have GDSM

### Armor for Rogues
Rogues can wear any armor. Unlike Wizards, there's no spell failure penalty for
heavy armor, so you can go for high AC freely.
- Leather armor → studded leather → ring mail → chain mail → splint mail → plate mail
- Or skip ahead with a wish to GDSM

### Items to collect
- **Daggers** — every single one you find; buy them from shops
- **Scrolls of enchant weapon** — enchant your dagger stack eventually
- **Rings of increase damage** — huge boost when combined with backstab multiplier
- **Wand of sleep** — enables reliable backstabbing on demand

## Stage 2: Gnomish Mines (branches off DL2–4)

**Goal:** Luckstone, Minetown supplies.

### Minetown for Rogues
- Rogues start with gold and Chaotic alignment, so the general store will sell to you normally
- Identify items through shop interaction
- The weapons shop often sells daggers — buy all of them
- The temple may be of a hostile alignment (Chaotic vs Lawful town) — proceed with caution;
  don't attack town guardians

### Rogue in the Mines
- Stealth makes the Mines significantly easier — many gnomes won't wake up until you're ready
- Backstab gnome lords and gnomish kings for easy XP
- Mines' End boss (gnomish king variant): may carry a war hammer or other weapon;
  backstab from stealth, throw daggers if needed

### Priority items from Mines
- **Luckstone** (as always)
- Any daggers you find lying around
- Gems from Mines' End — Rogues can sometimes identify gems by looking at them;
  use your touchstone (start item) to separate valuable from worthless

---

## Stage 3: Sokoban (branches off DL6–10)

**Goal:** Complete puzzles; either prize is good.

### Sokoban for Rogues
- No teleportation inside Sokoban; your normal escape tools don't work
- Stealth still functions — useful when monsters appear
- Backstab any monsters that wander in from off-level

### Prize priority for Rogues
- **Bag of holding** — very useful (Rogues carry lots of daggers + supplies)
- **Amulet of reflection** — also solid; Rogues often don't have another reflection source

---

## Stage 4: Your Quest (branches off DL7–10, requires XL10)

**Goal:** Retrieve the Master Key of Thievery from the Master Assassin in the Assassins' Guild Hall.

### Quest levels
- Talk to the Master of Thieves (quest leader) on the first quest level
- Several filler levels of dungeon-style play with rogue/assassin-themed monsters
- Final level: the Assassins' Guild Hall, guarded by the Master Assassin

### The nemesis: The Master Assassin
- Uses backstab attacks (fitting) — very dangerous if they hit
- Has high AC and decent HP
- Strategy: throw your full stack of enchanted daggers before getting into melee
- Or: use a wand of sleep to put them under, then backstab for massive damage
- The Guild Hall is full of assassin-type monsters; clear with dagger volleys

### The Master Key of Thievery
- **Carrying it:** passive stealth boost; may occasionally trigger random teleportation (teleportitis)
- **Invoking it:** teleports you with or without control depending on your ring of teleport control
- **Applying it:** opens any lock in the dungeon (doors, chests, containers) — no lockpick needed

### When to attempt the quest
- Recommended: XL12+, with at least a few enchanted daggers and some escape capability
- The quest monsters can backstab; wear full armor even if AC is high from cloak of displacement

## Stage 5: Mid-Game (DL10–30)

**Goal:** Enchant daggers to +5, max out AC, acquire critical resistances.

### Dagger investment
This is the stage where you collect and enchant your dagger arsenal:
1. Accumulate 8–15 daggers (or more)
2. Bless them (dip in holy water)
3. Enchant to +5 using scrolls of enchant weapon
   - Each enchant scroll raises the whole stack by +1 (confirm each one)
   - Stop at +5 (enchanting further risks destruction)
4. A stack of 8 blessed +5 daggers = your main combat ability for the rest of the game

### Wishing strategy
**Wish priority for Rogue:**
1. `blessed +2 gray dragon scale mail` — magic resistance; this is critical for late game
   (or silver dragon scale mail for reflection if you don't have it from Sokoban)
2. `blessed +2 speed boots` — speed is crucial
3. `blessed +2 gauntlets of dexterity` — boosts Dex, improves to-hit and backstab activation
4. Extra wishes: `blessed scroll of charging`, more enchants, `amulet of life saving`

### Critical resistances
- **Magic resistance** — GDSM or cloak of magic resistance; blocks instadeath
- **Poison resistance** — very important for Rogues (many dungeon animals are poisonous);
  try to eat a poisonous corpse safely (use ring of slow digestion)
- **Fire resistance** — needed for Gehennom
- **Reflection** — amulet or silver DSM

### The cloak trade-off
At some point you'll want to switch from cloak of displacement to cloak of magic resistance.
The right time: when you have GDSM (which already gives magic resistance), you no longer
need the cloak for magic resistance — you can wear an amulet of reflection + GDSM and keep
your cloak of displacement as an AC/evasion bonus.

Best setup:
- Body: GDSM (magic resistance + AC)
- Neck: amulet of reflection (or protection)
- Cloak: displacement (evasion) — you can keep it

## Stage 6: The Castle (approximately DL35)

**Goal:** Get the wand of wishing; clear the army of soldiers.

### Rogue approach to the Castle
- **Bell of Opening** lowers the drawbridge — use it on the gate
- Alternatively: dig through a wall from the side (wand of digging)
- Your dagger volley is excellent against groups of soldiers
- Throw 8+ daggers into a cluster of soldiers to thin the crowd
- Backstab isolated soldiers in corridors as you advance
- The castle dragon: use a full dagger volley or wand of death; don't let it breathe on you

### Using the Master Key of Thievery in the Castle
- Every chest and door in the Castle opens with the Master Key; no need to force them
- The main wand of wishing is in a chest — just apply the Key to open it
- Check all chests; there are often scrolls, rings, and wands beyond just the main chest

---

## Stage 7: Gehennom (approximately DL45–50)

**Goal:** Collect the three invocation items, survive the descent.

### Rogue in Gehennom
Rogues have unique tools for Gehennom:
- **Dagger volleys** clear most demon types quickly
- **Stealth** still works on some Gehennom monsters; backstab where possible
- **Master Key of Thievery** opens any locked door in the Gehennom fortresses
- **Teleportation** via invoking the Master Key — retreat when overwhelmed

### The Wizard of Yendor
- He'll start pursuing you in Gehennom
- He has magic resistance, so don't rely on wands of death
- Your dagger volley works well — throw all enchanted daggers, then collect and repeat
- Backstab if you can set it up: sleep wand, then dagger volley while he's sleeping

### Vlad's Tower
- Same as other roles: find Vlad, kill him, take the Candelabrum
- Rogues can sneak through Vlad's Tower corridors effectively (stealth)
- Vlad level-drains: carry potions of gain level to recover
- Backstab Vlad for big damage if you can approach unseen (difficult but possible)

### Gehennom levels to know
- **Asmodeus' Lair** — skip if possible; find stairs and move on
- **Juiblex's Swamp** — slime-type monsters; watch your inventory (acid destroys items)
- **Baalzebul's Lair** — skip if possible
- **Vlad's Tower** — must do for Candelabrum
- **Wizard of Yendor's area** — must kill him once for Book of the Dead

---

## Stage 8: The Invocation Ritual (Sanctum level)

**Goal:** Perform the ritual, kill the High Priest, take the Amulet.

### Rogue approach to the Sanctum
- Stealth helps in the approach — some monsters in the Sanctum may not detect you immediately
- Ring of conflict is invaluable here: activate it before entering and let demons fight each other
- The High Priest: throw your full enchanted dagger stack for burst damage
- Follow up in melee or with a wand of death (High Priest may have magic resistance)

### Invocation sequence (same for all roles)
While on the Vibrating Square:
1. **Ring the Bell of Opening** (`a`pply it)
2. **Invoke the Candelabrum** (must be lit, with 7 candles — `a`pply it)
3. **Read the Book of the Dead** (`r`ead it)

Then kill the High Priest and take the Amulet.

---

## Stage 9: The Ascent

**Goal:** Ascend from the Sanctum through all of Gehennom.

### Rogue ascent advantages
- **Master Key of Thievery invocation** gives teleportation — use it to skip past dangerous areas
- **Potions of gain level** skip entire levels of Gehennom
- Speed boots + intrinsic speed (if gained) = move twice per turn, escaping most pursuit
- Stealth still reduces random encounters

### Rogue ascent disadvantages
- No reliable way to clear the Wizard of Yendor without magic (unlike Wizard role)
- Must rely on dagger volleys and wands; conserve wand charges for the ascent

---

## Stage 10: The Elemental Planes

### Plane of Earth
- Wand of digging to clear through stone walls
- Lots of physical monsters; dagger volleys handle them
- Find and enter the portal to advance

### Plane of Air
- Rogues may lack levitation; check your inventory for a levitation ring or boots
- If you fall without levitation: cast a scroll of teleportation to reposition
- Air elementals: hit hard; throw daggers and retreat

### Plane of Water
- Water walking boots or levitation
- Rogues often have boots of speed rather than water walking; use a potion of levitation
- Electric eels and sea monsters: stay mobile, avoid clusters

### Plane of Fire
- Fire resistance non-negotiable
- Rogues should have fire resistance from a ring or GDSM
- Salamnaders and balrogs are very tough: stay at range, throw dagger volleys

### All planes
- Cast `scroll of magic mapping` (or use a wand) on every plane to see the full layout
- Find the portal and move; don't linger
- Save wand of death for any Riders you absolutely must deal with on the Astral Plane

---

## Stage 11: Astral Plane

**Goal:** Find the Chaotic altar, offer the Amulet, ascend.

### Rogue alignment
Rogues are Chaotic, so find the **Chaotic altar**.

### Astral strategy for Rogues
- Ring of conflict is your best friend here — activate it and monsters fight each other
- The Three Riders will be distracted by other monsters when conflict is running
- Navigate by throwing daggers to clear a path, then advancing to the Chaotic altar
- Backstab is still active: if a monster isn't targeting you yet, get that first hit backstab

### Handling the Three Riders
- **Death** — instakill touch; keep distance, use conflict to aim it at other monsters
- **Pestilence** — disease and stat drain; ring of conflict, ring of free action helps
- **Famine** — starvation; keep nutrition up (have food in inventory)
- Do NOT try to fight the Riders directly — even a Rogue can't win that fight

### Final steps
1. Navigate to the Chaotic altar
2. Kill the High Priest of Chaos guarding it
3. Apply (`a`) the Amulet of Yendor on the altar
4. **Ascension.**

## Common Deaths to Avoid

- **Early melee against tough monsters** — play stealthy; if you can't backstab, consider retreating
- **Forgetting to re-pick up daggers** after a throw — your thrown daggers land on the floor; collect them
- **Running into the Wizard of Yendor without magic resistance** — get GDSM first
- **Vlad's Tower underleveled** — level drain is crippling; have gain level potions before entering
- **The Three Riders on Astral without conflict active** — always have a ring of conflict charged
- **The High Priest killing you during the Invocation** — go in with full HP and high AC; have escape plan
- **Dying on the Planes without fire/shock/cold resistance** — check your resistances before each Plane

## Rogue vs Fighter vs Wizard: Quick Comparison

| | Fighter | Wizard | Rogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early survival | Easy | Hard | Medium |
| Main attack | Melee sword | Magic missile / finger of death | Backstab + dagger volley |
| HP | Very high | Low | Medium |
| AC achievable | Very high | Medium (spell penalty) | High |
| Special utility | Orb of Fate (half damage) | Map + detect + teleport | Stealth + lockpicking + teleport |
| Gehennom difficulty | Easy | Medium | Medium |
| Planes difficulty | Medium | Easy (magic map) | Medium |
| Overall difficulty | Easiest | Hardest | Medium |
