# NetHack Ascension Roadmap: Fighter (Valkyrie)

The Fighter (called Valkyrie in standard NetHack) is the best role for a first
or second ascension. Excellent combat stats, high starting HP, early intrinsics,
and a powerful quest artifact make this the most forgiving path to winning.

---

## Character Overview

**Alignment:** Neutral  
**Starting stats:** High Str and Con, decent Dex  
**Starting gear:** Long sword (+0), dagger, ring mail, small shield, food, touchstone  
**Pet:** None at start (may have a kitten in some versions)  
**Quest artifact:** Orb of Fate — half physical damage, acts as luckstone (+3 luck),
can be invoked for level teleport  

**Key intrinsics gained automatically by level:**
- Speed (fast movement) — around XL7
- Stealth — around XL11  
- Cold resistance — around XL14  

**Role strengths:**
- Best base HP growth of any role
- Can wield two weapons simultaneously at high level
- Starts with metal armor (ring mail), so AC is manageable immediately
- Cold resistance means no gear needed for that slot

## Stage 1: Early Dungeon (Dungeon Levels 1–6)

**Goal:** Survive, build HP, find basic equipment upgrades, identify useful scrolls/potions.

### What to do
- Identify your touchstone early — it identifies gem type by rubbing; sell or keep depending on what you find
- Fight everything at first; XP and HP growth are fast for Valkyrie/Fighter
- Look for armor upgrades: splint mail → plate mail → eventually dragon scale mail
- Collect scrolls of identify — use a stack of items then identify the scroll to name everything
- Find or buy a magic lamp if possible (Minetown shop); save it for a wish
- Pick up any wand of wishing immediately — it's the single most valuable item in the game
- AC target for this stage: below 5

### Items to watch for
- **Scrolls of enchant armor/weapon** — use on your main weapon and armor
- **Potions of gain level** — carry them; use strategically in Gehennom later
- **Rings of increase damage/accuracy** — upgrade combat significantly
- **Wand of cold/sleep** — good backup weapons early

### What to avoid
- Don't eat corpses randomly — many are poisonous or worse; check a spoiler or know your monsters
- Don't drink from fountains randomly — they can summon water demons, remove luck, or create monsters
- Don't read unidentified scrolls when confused; "scroll of fire" will burn your stuff

## Stage 2: Gnomish Mines (branches off DL2–4)

**Goal:** Reach Mines' End, get the luckstone, return.

The Mines branch off the main dungeon around levels 2–4. You'll see a staircase
down marked differently. The Mines are ~13 levels deep, ending at Mines' End.

### Key features
- **Minetown** (around Mines level 5–6): a town with shops and a temple
  - Buy a magic lamp here if available — rub it for a wish later
  - The temple priest can bless/uncurse items for a donation
  - Identify scrolls, potions, and rings using the shops (selling unidentified items reveals type)
  - Minetown may have a Watchman who will attack if you're hostile or wielding a weapon in the shop area
- **Mines' End** (bottom of Mines): several variants, always contains:
  - A gnomish king or similar boss
  - **A luckstone** — almost always on the floor. Pick it up. Blessed luckstone = +3 luck, critical for consistent play
  - Lots of gems scattered around (mostly worthless glass)

### Fighter/Valkyrie tips
- Ring mail handles Mines enemies fine early on
- Cold resistance helps with the cold-resistant gnomes later
- Don't dip your long sword in fountains here — Fighter/Valkyrie is Neutral, not Lawful, so Excalibur won't appear

### What to bring back
- **The luckstone** (blessed if possible — dip in holy water)
- Any useful potions, scrolls, or rings from shop purchases
- Return to main dungeon once you have the luckstone; don't linger

## Stage 3: Sokoban (branches off DL6–10)

**Goal:** Complete all 4 Sokoban puzzle levels, claim the prize.

Sokoban is a series of 4 puzzle levels where you push boulders into pits to create
paths. It branches off the main dungeon. Each level has one solution; there's no
combat required (though monsters appear).

### Rules
- You cannot teleport in Sokoban
- Boulders can only be pushed, not pulled
- Each level must be solved cleanly to advance

### The prize (top level of Sokoban)
One of two items — which one is random per game:
- **Bag of holding** — lets you carry enormous weight; almost essential for late game
- **Amulet of reflection** — reflects wands/rays/beams; protects against gaze attacks

Both are excellent. Bag of holding is generally more universally useful.

### Fighter/Valkyrie tips
- The Sokoban levels have a lot of projectile-throwing monsters (Sokoban has sleep/confusion gas traps)
- Wear your armor and fight normally when monsters appear
- Amulet of reflection complements a Fighter well since you'll be in melee often

## Stage 4: Your Quest (branches off DL7–10, requires XL10)

**Goal:** Complete the Fighter/Valkyrie quest, return with the Orb of Fate.

The quest becomes available once you reach experience level 10. Your quest leader
(the Norn for Valkyrie/Fighter) is found on the first quest level and gives you a
mission. You must be of good standing with your god (not too many prayer timeouts,
not excessively chaotic acts).

### Quest levels
- **Shrine of Destiny** — starting level, talk to the Norn
- **Filler levels** — several levels of dungeon-style play with Valkyrie-themed monsters
- **Cave of Surtur** — final level containing Lord Surtur, your nemesis

### The nemesis: Lord Surtur
- A powerful fire giant lord
- Carries the Orb of Fate
- Surrounded by fire giants and other dangerous creatures
- Strategy: teleport in, kill Surtur specifically, teleport out if overwhelmed

### The Orb of Fate
- Carrying it: provides half physical damage (very powerful passive defense), acts as a luckstone
- Invoking it: level teleports you toward the surface (useful escape)
- This is one of the best quest artifacts — carry it always after retrieval

### When to attempt the quest
- Recommended: XL14–16, with decent AC (below 0 ideally), and some way to handle fire (fire resistance or fire wand)
- Lord Surtur hits very hard; don't attempt underleveled

## Stage 5: Mid-Game (DL10–30)

**Goal:** Build up gear, get AC to -10 to -15, acquire critical items.

### Armor priority
1. **Gray dragon scale mail (GDSM)** — magic resistance + heavy AC. Best armor in the game.
   Get via wish if possible (see Wishing, below).
   Alternative: silver dragon scale mail (reflection) if you don't have an amulet of reflection yet.
2. **Enchant armor** to +5 (cap for most armor)
3. Stack AC bonuses from rings, cloaks, shield, boots, helm

### Wishing strategy
Wishes come from:
- Magic lamps (rub once = 1/3 chance, higher if blessed)
- Wands of wishing (3–4 charges normally)
- Fountains (rare — "a djinni appears and grants your wish")

**Wish priority for Fighter/Valkyrie:**
1. `blessed +2 gray dragon scale mail` — magic resistance is critical in late game
2. `blessed +2 speed boots` — if you haven't gained intrinsic speed yet
3. `blessed +2 gauntlets of power` — strength 25, enables two-weapon fighting and heavy lifting
4. Extra wishes: `blessed scroll of charging` to recharge the wand of wishing

### Weapon development
- Long sword → enchant to +5 via scrolls of enchant weapon
- Find or wish for **Mjollnir** (requires Str 25, deals massive shock damage — excellent)
- Or: keep the long sword and find a good secondary weapon for two-weapon combat

### Critical resistances to acquire
- **Magic resistance** — from GDSM or cloak of magic resistance; blocks instadeath from wands/spells
- **Poison resistance** — from eating a poisonous corpse (ring of slow digestion first to eat safely), or ring/cloak
- **Fire resistance** — needed for Gehennom; ring, boots, or dragon scale mail
- **Shock resistance** — useful for Gehennom
- **Reflection** — from amulet or silver DSM; reflects beams

### The Oracle
- Found around DL5–9, a special level with a large circular room
- You can pay the Oracle for hints (minor = 50 gold, major = 500 gold)
- Major Oracle consultations give useful strategic hints; can do up to 3
- The Oracle level is also a good place to find potions of gain level

## Stage 6: The Castle (approximately DL35)

**Goal:** Lower the drawbridge, claim the wand of wishing, descend.

The Castle is a special level: a large stone fortress with a drawbridge, guarded
by an army of soldiers and a sleeping dragon.

### Entering the Castle
- The main entrance has a drawbridge, locked with a portcullis
- Use the **Bell of Opening** (found somewhere in the dungeon, often in a chest) to lower the drawbridge
- Alternatives: dig through a wall, fly over the moat, or teleport past the gate
- Note: The castle has a "no teleport" zone near the entrance in some configurations

### Inside the Castle
- Hundreds of soldiers, captains, sergeants, and a dragon
- Lots of weapons and armor to pick up
- **The wand of wishing** is in a chest in the center of the castle (almost always)
  - Blessed/identify the chest before opening (may be trapped)
  - The wand typically has 3–4 charges; use them wisely

### After the Castle
- Descend one level to the **Valley of the Dead** — undead-heavy level, not too dangerous
- Below is **Gehennom**

## Stage 7: Gehennom (approximately DL45–50)

**Goal:** Find the three invocation items, survive the descent to the Sanctum.

Gehennom is the endgame dungeon. Multiple special levels, increasing difficulty.
The Wizard of Yendor patrols here and is very dangerous.

### Special Gehennom levels (in rough order of depth)
- **Asmodeus' Lair** — a demon lord's fortress, fire + cold resistant demons
- **Juiblex's Swamp** — acid and slime monsters
- **Baalzebul's Lair** — another demon lord's lair
- **Vlad's Tower** — a 3-level mini-branch
- **Wizard of Yendor's Tower** — the recurring boss's home
- **Sanctum** — the final level

### The Wizard of Yendor
- Will attack you repeatedly once you enter Gehennom
- Carries the **Book of the Dead** (one of the three invocation items)
- When first killed: drops the Book. But the Wizard keeps coming back.
- When he resurrects, he takes the Book back — you must kill him again each time
- **Strategy:** Kill him as quickly as possible; drain life, magic attacks, or brute force

### Vlad's Tower (branch in Gehennom)
- 3 levels, heavily trapped, lots of undead (vampires, vampire lords)
- **Vlad the Impaler** (boss) carries the **Candelabrum of Invocation**
- Vlad can drain levels — bring a way to restore levels (potion of gain level)
- Strategy: can sometimes steal the Candelabrum via scroll of taming + conflict, or just kill him directly
- After getting the Candelabrum: attach 7 candles (just apply the candelabrum with candles in inventory)

### The three invocation items
1. **Bell of Opening** — found earlier in the dungeon
2. **Candelabrum of Invocation** — from Vlad the Impaler (Vlad's Tower)
3. **Book of the Dead** — from the Wizard of Yendor (first kill drops it)

## Stage 8: The Invocation Ritual (Sanctum level)

**Goal:** Perform the ritual, kill the High Priest, take the Amulet.

### The Sanctum
- The deepest level of the dungeon
- Contains: the High Priest of Moloch, many dangerous demons, and the Vibrating Square
- The High Priest is wearing the Amulet of Yendor around his neck (wielded as a weapon? no — carried)

### The Vibrating Square
- A special tile marked differently (often blinking `_`)
- Stand on it to perform the Invocation

### Performing the Invocation
While standing on the Vibrating Square, in order:
1. **Ring the Bell of Opening** (apply it)
2. **Invoke the Candelabrum** (lit, with all 7 candles — apply it)
3. **Read the Book of the Dead** (r-ead it)

This summons the High Priest and opens the way.

### The High Priest
- Extremely dangerous: multiple attacks, carries the Amulet of Yendor
- Has magic resistance, can call for help from demons
- Strategy for Fighter/Valkyrie: go in at full HP, use conflict (ring of conflict makes all monsters
  fight each other — very useful here), brute-force kill the High Priest directly
- Once dead, take the **Amulet of Yendor** from their inventory
- Wear or carry the Amulet — you are now racing to ascend

## Stage 9: The Ascent (Amulet in hand)

**Goal:** Ascend from the deepest level through all of Gehennom and the main dungeon to the top.

### What changes once you have the Amulet
- The Wizard of Yendor now pursues you aggressively and repeatedly
- Every 50 turns spent in Gehennom summons 2–3 random demons
- Moving upward is now the primary goal — don't dawdle

### Surviving the ascent
- Use **potions of gain level** (level teleport upward) to skip levels of Gehennom
- Teleportation generally works in Gehennom (unlike some branches)
- If the Wizard steals the Amulet: kill him again immediately to recover it
- Speed is your friend — intrinsic speed + speed boots = moving two steps per turn

### Shortcuts back through the dungeon
- You collected the Orb of Fate during your quest — invoke it to level-teleport toward surface
- Portals in Gehennom connect some levels (you passed through these going down)

## Stage 10: The Elemental Planes

**Goal:** Pass through 4 elemental planes and the Astral Plane to ascend.

After ascending past the Castle with the Amulet, you reach a portal to the
**Plane of Earth**. The planes must be crossed in order: Earth → Air → Water → Fire → Astral.

On each plane, you must find the portal to the next plane (it's hidden on the level).

### Plane of Earth
- Mostly solid rock; you must dig your way through to find the portal
- Bring a wand of digging or pickaxe
- Monsters: xorns (move through walls), earth elementals, stone giants
- **Fire resistance needed** if you don't have it; lava in some areas
- Find and enter the portal (glowing staircase) to advance

### Plane of Air
- No solid ground; everything is floating or flying
- You may fall if you don't have levitation or flying
- Monsters: air elementals, djinn, vortices
- Very confusing layout — use teleportation or just wander until you find the portal
- Wear **slow digestion ring** if you haven't eaten recently (food is scarce)

### Plane of Water
- The entire level is water; you need either water walking (boots), levitation, or swimming
- Monsters: water elementals, sea monsters, electric eels
- **Cold resistance and shock resistance** strongly recommended
- The portal is somewhere in the water — find it while navigating carefully

### Plane of Fire
- Everything is on fire; fire resistance is non-negotiable here
- Monsters: fire elementals, salamanders, balrogs (very tough)
- **Fire resistance** absolutely required; cold attacks damage fire monsters
- The portal is somewhere in the lava fields

### Tips for all planes
- Keep a scroll of teleportation handy to reposition
- The Amulet of Yendor prevents you from teleporting freely on the Astral Plane
  (on the planes before Astral you can still teleport)
- Save your wand of death / finger of death for the Riders on the Astral Plane

## Stage 11: Astral Plane

**Goal:** Find your god's altar, offer the Amulet of Yendor, ascend.

### Layout
- Three large rooms, each containing an altar of a different alignment (Lawful, Neutral, Chaotic)
- Crowded with monsters including the Three Riders

### The Three Riders
Extremely dangerous unique monsters:
- **Death** — instakills with a touch; can only be kept at bay; magic resistance helps
- **Pestilence** — causes disease and stat drain; also very dangerous
- **Famine** — causes starvation; drains nutrition

**Strategy:** Do NOT try to kill the Riders. You can't permanently kill Death or Famine
(they resurrect immediately). Run past them or use conflict to keep them busy.

### Finding your altar
- Fighter/Valkyrie is Neutral → find the **Neutral altar**
- The altar alignment is shown in the description when you look at it (`l`ook)
- High priests guard each altar — you must kill the priest at your alignment altar

### Ascending
1. Navigate to the Neutral altar (for Fighter/Valkyrie)
2. Kill the High Priest of your alignment
3. Stand on the altar
4. Apply (`a`) the Amulet of Yendor to offer it
5. **You ascend. The game is won.**

## Common Deaths to Avoid

- **Wand of death from a soldier in the Castle** — magic resistance prevents this; get GDSM first
- **Lord Surtur before you're ready** — don't do the quest at XL10, wait until XL14+
- **The Wizard of Yendor drains all your levels** — use conflict or kill him fast
- **Drowning on Plane of Water** — have a way to traverse water before entering
- **Death touching you on Astral** — never let Death get adjacent; conflict or teleport away
