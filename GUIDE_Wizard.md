# NetHack Ascension Roadmap: Wizard

The Wizard is a glass cannon in the early game but arguably the most powerful role
in the late game. You start fragile — low AC, mediocre HP — but your spell library,
magic resistance from your starting cloak, and ability to identify items quickly
make you extremely self-sufficient once established.

---

## Character Overview

**Alignment:** Neutral or Chaotic (your choice at start)  
**Starting stats:** Very high Int, low Str and Con  
**Starting gear:** Quarterstaff (+0), spellbook of magic missile, cloak of magic resistance,
ring of slow digestion (or similar), food  
**Pet:** Kitten (small cat — becomes a housecat, then a large cat over time)  
**Quest artifact:** Eye of the Aethiopica — when carried, allows casting any spell regardless
of spell failure rate; when invoked, restores magical energy (Pw)  

**Key intrinsics gained automatically by level:**
- Energy regeneration improvements — Pw recovers faster as you level
- Wizard has the highest starting and max Pw of any role

**Role strengths:**
- Magic resistance at character creation (from starting cloak) — huge early advantage
- Can identify items from spell casting (read unidentified books = identify or gain spell)
- Best spell selection in the game
- Can reliably teleport, escape, and control the battlefield

## Stage 1: Early Dungeon (Dungeon Levels 1–6)

**Goal:** Survive — this is the hardest part of a Wizard game. You are weak and fragile.

### The fragile early game
- Your AC is terrible at start (cloak is AC 1 or so, no other armor)
- Your quarterstaff does minimal damage
- Magic missile is your main offense early on — 2d6+level damage, costs 5 Pw
- You die easily; play very carefully

### What to do immediately
- Keep the cloak of magic resistance on at all times — it's irreplaceable early
- Cast magic missile from range; don't melee
- Identify your starting ring — ring of slow digestion is common Wizard start, which lets
  you eat dangerous corpses safely (highly valuable)
- Find or buy leather armor or any body armor ASAP — Wizard can wear any armor but spells
  fail at a higher rate in heavy metal armor (splint mail etc.)
- Soft armor (leather, studded leather, elven, robe) is preferred so spells stay castable
- Your pet kitten can level up to be a genuinely useful fighter early on — let it fight

### Spell priorities
Cast these early, identify them, build your library:
1. **Magic missile** — your starting spell; main ranged attack early
2. **Force bolt** — if you find the book; destroys boulders, damages monsters
3. **Protection** — improves your AC; extremely important for early game survival
   - Cast protection repeatedly while Pw recharges; builds toward max AC bonus
4. **Identify** — if you find the book; the single most useful non-combat spell
5. **Detect treasure** — find scrolls, potions, wands on each level

### Armor note for Wizards
Heavy metal armor (plate mail, splint mail, ring mail) increases spell failure rates
significantly. **Preferred armor options:**
- Robe (if found) — custom Wizard armor, doesn't penalize spells
- Leather armor / studded leather — acceptable, light spell penalty
- Elven mithril coat — if you find it; excellent and light
- Dragon scale mail later (acceptable despite being metal; GDSM is worth the penalty)

### Items to watch for
- **Spellbooks of any kind** — read them all (safely, when not confused or blind)
- **Wands of any kind** — Wizards recharge wands more reliably than other roles
- **Scrolls of identify** — use them constantly; knowing what you have is half the game
- **Potions of gain level** — save for Gehennom
- **Potions of full healing** — use when in trouble, save a few for later

## Stage 2: Gnomish Mines (branches off DL2–4)

**Goal:** Luckstone, Minetown shopping, return.

### Minetown for Wizards
- **Identify scrolls/potions/rings at the shops** — selling unidentified items to a shop
  tells you the item type; you can then buy back at full price or just note the type
- Buy spellbooks if available — every new spell is a capability
- Donate to a temple (give gold) to improve your god relationship and get free blessed water
- The **magic lamp** (if stocked in a shop) is worth buying — rubbing gives a wish

### Mines' End
- Same as any role: find the luckstone, bless it if possible
- The gnomish king level can be tricky at Wizard's low HP; don't overstay

### What Wizards should prioritize from the Mines
- **Luckstone** (blessed)
- Any uncursed or blessed scrolls from Minetown shops
- Wands (recharge them later for extra charges)

## Stage 3: Sokoban (branches off DL6–10)

**Goal:** Bag of holding strongly preferred over amulet of reflection.

Wizards accumulate enormous numbers of spellbooks and items. The bag of holding is
almost mandatory for a Wizard because your inventory weight will be crippling
without it. Prioritize this branch early.

### Wizard-specific Sokoban notes
- You can cast **teleport** but it is blocked inside Sokoban; don't rely on it here
- Cast **protection** between puzzle levels while Pw regenerates
- The puzzle levels themselves are purely logic — no spell advantage applies

### Prize priority
If you get **amulet of reflection** instead of bag of holding:
- You'll need to find a container (large box, sack) early to manage weight
- Amulet of reflection is still extremely good — wish for bag of holding later if needed

## Stage 4: Your Quest (branches off DL7–10, requires XL10)

**Goal:** Retrieve the Eye of the Aethiopica from the Dark One in the Tower of Darkness.

### Quest levels
- Talk to your quest leader first (the Enchanter on the first quest level)
- Must be at character level 10 and in good standing with your alignment

### The nemesis: The Dark One
- A powerful mage-type enemy in the Tower of Darkness
- Can cast spells including death touch attacks
- Has magic resistance, so your spells may not all land
- Strategy: force bolt to break apart any walls, polymorph monsters away with wand of polymorph,
  cast finger of death if you have it, or drain their HP with magic missile spam
- The Dark One's Tower has teleport traps — useful for escape

### The Eye of the Aethiopica
- **Carrying it:** spells never fail due to lack of skill/training (you can cast at full spell list
  even in non-preferred schools)
- **Invoking it:** restores Pw (spell points) immediately — invaluable in late game
- Combined with high Int and your natural Pw pool, you become essentially a casting machine

### When to attempt
- Recommended: XL12–14, with some way to kill the Dark One (finger of death, force bolt spam,
  or a good wand)
- Get the Eye as early as possible — it transforms your casting capability

## Stage 5: Mid-Game (DL10–30)

**Goal:** Expand your spell library, achieve stable AC, become self-sufficient.

### Spell library priorities
Collect and learn these spells (in rough priority order):

| Spell | School | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Identify | Divination | Name every item; essential |
| Detect treasure | Divination | Find items on each level |
| Teleport | Escape | Get out of trouble; controlled with Int |
| Wizard lock | Matter | Lock/unlock doors; trap corridors |
| Cancellation | Matter | Remove monster enchantments, cancel wands |
| Polymorph | Matter | Transform monsters or yourself |
| Protection | Protection | Build AC bonus |
| Cure sickness | Healing | Remove disease |
| Finger of death | Attack | Instakill (fail-based vs magic-resistant) |
| Magic mapping | Divination | Reveal full level |
| Stone to flesh | Healing | Reverse petrification |

### Wishing strategy
**Wish priority for Wizard:**
1. `blessed +2 gray dragon scale mail` — magic resistance AND heavy AC; spell failure goes up
   but it's worth it in late game (use Ring of gain energy if Pw is pinched)
   Alternative if spell failure is too costly: `blessed +2 silver dragon scale mail` (reflection)
   and get magic resistance from another source
2. `blessed +2 speed boots` — speed is crucial
3. `blessed +2 cloak of magic resistance` — if your starting cloak is lost or destroyed
4. Extra wishes: `blessed scroll of charging`, more spellbooks, or `blessed amulet of life saving`

### Teleportation control
- Wizards with high Int can teleport to specific levels (controlled teleportation)
- A ring of teleport control gives reliable controlled teleport even without high Int
- Use teleport to escape sticky situations; this is your primary survival tool mid-game

### Building Pw (spell points)
- Your Pw pool is very large as a Wizard
- Pw regenerates over time (faster at higher levels)
- Invoking the Eye of the Aethiopica restores Pw in an emergency
- Wands of energy can also restore Pw if you have them

## Stage 6: The Castle (approximately DL35)

**Goal:** Get the wand of wishing; use your spells and wands to clear the castle.

### Wizard approach to the Castle
- You don't need the Bell of Opening to enter — you can:
  - Dig through a wall (wand of digging or pickaxe)
  - Teleport inside
  - Use a scroll of earth to drop boulders, or just float over the moat
- Finger of death is devastating against soldiers if they're not magic resistant
- Sleep, paralysis, and confusion spells work on soldiers; use crowd control
- The castle dragon (usually sleeping) — wake it up only if you want to fight it for its scales

### After the Castle
- Same as all roles: Valley of the Dead (one level), then into Gehennom
- Consider: `blessed scroll of teleportation` lets you skip ahead in the dungeon

## Stage 7: Gehennom (approximately DL45–50)

**Goal:** Collect all three invocation items; survive the increasingly dangerous demonic levels.

### Wizard-specific advantages in Gehennom
- **Finger of death** instakills most Gehennom demons (those without magic resistance)
- **Cancellation** removes dangerous enchantments from monsters
- **Teleportation** lets you escape bad situations quickly
- **Detect treasure** reveals the candelabrum in Vlad's Tower efficiently

### The Wizard of Yendor — your recurring nemesis
The Wizard of Yendor is especially annoying for a Wizard player character because
both use similar attacks. Key notes:
- He has magic resistance, so finger of death will often fail
- Force bolt, physical attacks, or wand of death are more reliable
- His spells can confuse, paralyze, and drain you
- Kill him immediately on each encounter — he has the Book of the Dead

### Gehennom levels to know
- **Asmodeus' Lair:** Asmodeus the demon lord; can be bypassed (just find stairs and move on)
- **Juiblex's Swamp:** acid monsters; Juiblex himself is dangerous but not required
- **Baalzebul's Lair:** Baalzebul the demon lord; again, just find stairs
- **Vlad's Tower:** see below
- **Wizard's Tower:** the recurring boss; collect the Book of the Dead

### Vlad's Tower
- 3 levels of undead-heavy dungeon
- Lots of vampires — they drain experience levels
- Carry potions of gain level to restore lost levels immediately
- Vlad the Impaler has the Candelabrum; he also has level-drain attacks
- Wizard strategy: finger of death, then immediately grab the Candelabrum before moving on

## Stage 8: The Invocation Ritual (Sanctum level)

**Goal:** Perform the ritual, kill the High Priest, take the Amulet.

### Wizard approach to the Sanctum
- You have powerful crowd control (polymorph, sleep, cancellation)
- Cast **magic mapping** when you enter to see the full layout immediately
- Use **detect treasure** to find the Vibrating Square if needed
- The High Priest can cast spells — use conflict (ring of conflict) to distract surrounding demons
- Finger of death on the High Priest — he may have magic resistance, so prepare physical backup

### Invocation sequence (same for all roles)
While on the Vibrating Square:
1. **Ring the Bell of Opening** (`a`pply it)
2. **Invoke the Candelabrum** (must be lit, with 7 candles — `a`pply it)
3. **Read the Book of the Dead** (`r`ead it)

Then kill the High Priest and take the Amulet.

---

## Stage 9: The Ascent

**Goal:** Ascend from the Sanctum through Gehennom and the main dungeon.

### Wizard ascent advantages
- **Teleportation** is your biggest asset — can skip multiple Gehennom levels
- Cast **magic mapping** on each level to find exits quickly
- **Finger of death** clears any blocking demons
- Invoke the Eye of the Aethiopica to refill Pw for sustained casting during the ascent

### Managing the Wizard of Yendor on ascent
- He will attempt to steal the Amulet — kill him on sight every time
- If he steals the Amulet: cast teleport to relocate, hunt him down, kill him
- He cannot die permanently until you ascend — this is a design feature; just keep killing him

---

## Stage 10: The Elemental Planes

Planes are the same for all roles, but Wizard has specific advantages:

### Plane of Earth
- Wand of digging or stone to flesh spell to clear through walls
- Magic mapping reveals the full plane layout immediately — use this every time
- Cast detect treasure to find the portal

### Plane of Air
- Levitation is very helpful; a levitation ring or potion works
- Cast magic mapping; the portal is floating somewhere
- Air elementals: electrical attacks; shock resistance recommended

### Plane of Water
- Water walking boots, levitation, or very careful movement
- Magic mapping immediately on entry to plan your path
- Your spells work normally underwater; this is one of few advantages

### Plane of Fire
- Fire resistance absolutely required
- Cold-damage spells work wonderfully here (cone of cold, frost wand)
- Polymorph powerful monsters into something harmless

### Tips for all Planes
- Cast **magic mapping** the moment you enter each plane — this is Wizard's biggest plane advantage
- Use **detect treasure** to pinpoint the portal
- Teleport to reposition at will (except on Astral)

---

## Stage 11: Astral Plane

**Goal:** Find the Neutral altar, offer the Amulet, ascend.

### Wizard alignment
Wizard typically plays Neutral, so find the **Neutral altar**.
If you played Chaotic, find the **Chaotic altar**.

### Handling the Three Riders
- **Do not fight the Riders** — particularly Death (instakill touch) and Famine
- Finger of death is technically useful against Pestilence but risks backfire
- Use **ring of conflict** to make all monsters fight each other; the Riders will target nearby monsters
- **Teleport** to move through the Astral Plane quickly, bypassing the Riders
- Maintain magic resistance (GDSM or cloak) — prevents many of the Riders' magic effects

### Final steps
1. Navigate to the Neutral (or your alignment) altar
2. Kill the High Priest guarding that altar
3. Apply (`a`) the Amulet of Yendor on the altar
4. You ascend

## Common Deaths to Avoid

- **Early game melee** — don't fight in melee; stay back and magic missile
- **Losing your cloak of magic resistance** — if disenchanted or destroyed, replace immediately
- **Spell failure on Plane of Fire without fire resistance** — get fire resistance before the planes
- **The Three Riders on Astral** — never let Death get adjacent; keep conflict active
- **Running out of Pw during ascent** — invoke the Eye; carry potions of full healing as backup
- **The Wizard of Yendor stealing the Amulet while you're low on HP** — keep HP high during ascent
